Word Counter – Count Words Online

Count words and characters instantly with this free online word counter. Perfect for essays, articles, assignments, posts and everyday text.

What Is a Word Counter?

A word counter is an online tool that calculates the number of words and characters in a piece of text. It can be useful when writing articles, essays, assignments, blog posts, social media content and other documents with specific word or character limits.

How to Use This Word Counter

Using the ALTools Word Counter is simple. Type or paste your text into the box above and click the Count Words button. The tool will instantly display the total number of words and characters in your text.

What Does It Count?

The tool counts words separated by spaces and calculates the total number of characters in the text. It works directly in your browser, so you can quickly check your text without installing any software.

Why Use an Online Word Counter?

A word counter can help you stay within writing limits and quickly check the length of your content. Students can use it for assignments, writers can check articles, and content creators can use it for posts and other online content.
