What Is BMI?

BMI, or Body Mass Index, is a simple measurement based on a person's weight and height. It is commonly used as a general screening indicator for adults. BMI is not a medical diagnosis and does not directly measure body fat.

BMI Formula: How to Calculate BMI

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared:

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²

BMI Calculation Example

A person who weighs 70 kg and is 170 cm (1.70 m) tall has a BMI of 70 ÷ (1.70 × 1.70) = 70 ÷ 2.89 = 24.2. This falls in the normal weight range.

Limits of BMI

BMI uses only weight and height, so it cannot tell muscle from fat or show where fat is stored. Athletes and very muscular people can have a high BMI with low body fat, and older adults can have a normal BMI with little muscle. The ranges above are the standard adult ranges. Some health guidelines for Asian populations use lower cut-offs, for example treating a BMI of 23 or more as overweight, because health risks can rise at a lower BMI. Frequently Asked Questions

What does BMI stand for?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a measurement calculated using a person's weight and height.

How do I calculate BMI from kg and cm?

Divide your weight in kilograms by your height in metres squared. For example, 70 kg and 170 cm gives 70 ÷ (1.70 × 1.70) = 24.2, which is in the normal range.

How do I calculate BMI in pounds and inches?

Multiply your weight in pounds by 703, then divide by your height in inches squared. To use this calculator, convert pounds to kg by dividing by 2.205, and inches to cm by multiplying by 2.54.

What is a healthy BMI for adults?

A BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is generally considered the normal or healthy weight range for adults.

Is BMI calculated the same way for men and women?

Yes. For adults the formula and the category ranges are the same for men and women. Women often carry more body fat than men at the same BMI, so BMI is only a rough guide.

Is BMI accurate for athletes and muscular people?

Not always. BMI uses only weight and height, so it cannot tell muscle from fat. A muscular person can have a high BMI without having too much body fat.

Are BMI categories different for Asian people?

The standard ranges on this page are the general adult ranges. Some health guidelines for Asian populations use lower cut-offs, for example treating a BMI of 23 or more as overweight, because health risks can rise at a lower BMI. Ask your doctor which ranges suit you.

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. BMI is a general screening measurement and should not be considered a medical diagnosis.
