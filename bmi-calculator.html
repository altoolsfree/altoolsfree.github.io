What Is BMI?

BMI, or Body Mass Index, is a simple measurement based on a person's weight and height. It is commonly used as a general screening indicator for adults. BMI is not a medical diagnosis and does not directly measure body fat.

How to Calculate BMI

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared. This online BMI calculator performs the calculation automatically after you enter your height and weight.

BMI Categories for Adults

For adults, BMI is commonly grouped into general categories. A BMI below 18.5 is generally classified as underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 as normal weight, 25.0 to 29.9 as overweight, and 30.0 or higher as obese.

These categories are general screening ranges and may not be appropriate for every individual. Age, muscle mass, body composition, and other factors can affect how BMI should be interpreted.
