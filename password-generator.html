What makes a password strong?

  • Length: every extra character makes a password much harder to guess. Aim for 12 or more.
  • Mix of characters: use uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols together.
  • Randomness: avoid names, birthdays and common words. A random password has no pattern to guess.
  • Uniqueness: use a different password for every account.

Tips to keep your accounts safe

  • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) for email, banking and social media.
  • Use a password manager to store your passwords.
  • Never share your password by message or email.
  • Change a password right away if you think it was leaked.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a strong password be?

Use at least 12 characters. For important accounts such as email and banking, use 16 or more characters with a mix of letters, numbers and symbols.

Is this password generator safe?

Yes. Passwords are created in your browser using the secure random generator built into your device. They are never sent to a server or stored.

Should I reuse the same password?

No. Use a different password for every account. A password manager can help you remember them.
