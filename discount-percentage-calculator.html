What Is a Discount Percentage?

A discount percentage tells you how much is taken off the original price of a product or service. A 20% discount means you pay 20 parts less out of every 100 parts of the original price.

Discount Formula

  • Amount saved = Original Price × Discount % ÷ 100
  • Final price = Original Price − Amount Saved
  • Discount % = (Original Price − Sale Price) ÷ Original Price × 100

Discount Calculation Examples

Say a jacket costs 80 and the store offers 25% off. The saving is 80 × 25 ÷ 100 = 20, so the final price is 80 − 20 = 60.

If a phone was 500 and is now 425, the discount is (500 − 425) ÷ 500 × 100 = 15%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a discount percentage?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100 to get the savings. Subtract the savings from the original price to get the final price.

How do I find the discount percentage from two prices?

Subtract the sale price from the original price, divide the result by the original price, then multiply by 100.

What is 25% off 80?

25% of 80 is 20, so the final price is 80 − 20 = 60.
