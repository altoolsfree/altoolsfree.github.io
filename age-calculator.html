What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online tool that calculates a person's chronological age from their date of birth. This free age calculator shows your age in years, months, and days based on today's date.

How to Calculate Your Age

To calculate your exact age, select your date of birth and click the Calculate Age button. The calculator compares your birth date with the current date and displays your age in years, months, and days.

How to Use This Age Calculator

  • Select your date of birth from the date field.
  • Click the Calculate Age button.
  • Your exact chronological age will appear below the button.

Why Use an Online Age Calculator?

Calculating age manually can require checking different month lengths and leap years. An online age calculator makes the process quick and simple, helping you calculate your age without doing the calculation yourself.
