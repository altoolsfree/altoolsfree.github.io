Unit Converter – Convert Units Online

Convert kilometers to miles and miles to kilometers quickly with this free online unit converter.

What Is a Unit Converter?

A unit converter is an online tool that helps you convert a measurement from one unit to another. This ALTools converter currently supports kilometers to miles and miles to kilometers conversions.

Kilometers to Miles Converter

To convert kilometers to miles, enter a value in kilometers, select Kilometers → Miles, and click the Convert button. The result will be displayed instantly.

Kilometers to Miles Formula

1 kilometer is approximately equal to 0.621371 miles. To convert kilometers to miles, multiply the kilometer value by 0.621371.

Miles to Kilometers Converter

To convert miles to kilometers, enter a value in miles, select Miles → Kilometers, and click Convert. The result will appear immediately.

Miles to Kilometers Formula

1 mile is equal to approximately 1.609344 kilometers. To convert miles to kilometers, multiply the mile value by 1.609344.

How to Use This Unit Converter

Using the ALTools Unit Converter is simple. Enter your number, choose the type of conversion, and click the Convert button. The tool provides the converted value instantly.
