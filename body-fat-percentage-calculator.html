How to measure correctly

  • Height: stand straight without shoes.
  • Neck: measure just below the larynx (Adam's apple), with the tape slightly sloping down toward the front.
  • Waist: men measure at the navel. Women measure at the narrowest part of the waist. Breathe out normally and do not pull the stomach in.
  • Hip (women): measure around the widest part of the hips and buttocks.

Take each measurement twice and use the average. Measure in the morning before eating for the most consistent results.

Body fat percentage formulas

U.S. Navy method (metric, cm)

Men:
BF% = 86.010 × log10(waist - neck)
      - 70.041 × log10(height) + 36.76

Women:
BF% = 163.205 × log10(waist + hip - neck)
      - 97.684 × log10(height) - 78.387

BMI method

BF% = (1.20 × BMI) + (0.23 × age)
      - (10.8 × sex) - 5.4

sex = 1 for men
sex = 0 for women

The Navy method uses body measurements, while the BMI method estimates body fat using BMI and age. The BMI method can be less accurate for very muscular people.

Example

A man is 175 cm tall with a 38 cm neck and an 85 cm waist. Using the U.S. Navy formula, his estimated body fat is about 23.5%. This falls within the average range for men according to the chart above.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

For men, about 14-24% is considered fitness to average. For women, about 21-31% is considered fitness to average. Essential fat is about 2-5% for men and 10-13% for women.

How accurate is the Navy method?

The U.S. Navy method provides an estimate using body measurements. It is useful for tracking changes over time but is not a medical measurement.

Where should I measure my waist?

Men measure at the navel. Women measure at the narrowest point of the waist. Measure the neck just below the larynx and the hip at the widest point.

How often should I check my body fat percentage?

Once every 2-4 weeks is enough. Measure at the same time of day and under the same conditions each time.

This calculator gives an estimate for general information only. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor about your health goals.
