How to calculate average percentage

To calculate the average percentage, add all the percentages together and divide the total by the number of percentages. For example, the average of 70%, 80% and 90% is (70 + 80 + 90) / 3 = 80%.

  1. Write down all the percentages.
  2. Add them together.
  3. Divide the sum by how many percentages you added.

Average percentage formula

Average = (sum of all percentages) / (number of percentages)

Example: 70%, 80% and 90%. The sum is 240. Divide by 3 to get 80%.

Average of two percentages

To find the average of two percentages, add them and divide by 2. For 60% and 80%, the average is (60 + 80) / 2 = 70%. This works when both percentages come from the same total. If the totals are different, see the section further below.

Weighted average percentage

Use this when some items count more than others, for example an exam worth 60% of the grade and homework worth 40%.

Weighted average = (p1 x w1 + p2 x w2 + ...) / (w1 + w2 + ...)

Example: exam 85% with weight 60, homework 70% with weight 40. (85 x 60 + 70 x 40) / 100 = 79%.

Do not average percentages from different totals

Say you got 5 out of 10 on a quiz (50%) and 80 out of 100 on a test (80%). The simple average of 50% and 80% is 65%. But your real overall score is 85 out of 110, which is 77.3%. When totals are different, use the Marks to % mode.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate average percentage?

Add all the percentages together and divide by how many there are. For 70%, 80% and 90%, the sum is 240 and the average is 240 / 3 = 80%.

What is the formula for average percentage?

Average percentage = (sum of all percentages) / (number of percentages). For marks, use overall percentage = (total marks obtained / total maximum marks) x 100.

How do I calculate the average percentage of 5 subjects?

If every subject is out of 100, add the five marks and divide by 5. For example, 72, 85, 64, 90 and 78 add up to 389, and 389 / 5 = 77.8%. If the subjects have different totals, add all marks and all totals first, then divide and multiply by 100.

How do I find the average of two percentages?

Add the two percentages and divide by 2. For 60% and 80%, the average is (60 + 80) / 2 = 70%. This works when both percentages come from the same total.

What is a weighted average percentage?

A weighted average gives each percentage a different importance. Multiply each percentage by its weight, add the results, then divide by the total weight.

Can I just average percentages from different totals?

Not always. If the percentages come from different totals, add the marks and totals first. For example, 5 out of 10 and 80 out of 100 gives 85 out of 110, which is 77.3%, not the simple average of 65%.

How do I find my average percentage from marks?

Add your marks from all subjects, add the total marks of all subjects, divide the first by the second and multiply by 100.

Is an average percent calculator the same as a percentage average calculator?

Yes. Average percent calculator, percentage average calculator and average percentage calculator all mean the same tool. It finds the average of several percentages or your overall percentage from marks.
