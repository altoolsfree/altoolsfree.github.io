Why 16 characters?

Sixteen characters is a popular sweet spot. It is long enough to be very hard to crack, and short enough that most websites accept it. With all four character types, a random 16 character password has about 100 bits of strength.

16 characters vs. 8 or 12

LengthPossible combinations (all character types)
8 charactersabout 2 quadrillion
12 charactersabout 10 sextillion times more than 8
16 charactersbillions of billions times more than 12

Each added character multiplies the number of possible passwords by about 86. That is why length is the biggest factor.

If a website rejects your password

  • Turn off Symbols. Some sites only allow letters and numbers.
  • Turn on Avoid look-alikes if you need to type the password by hand.
  • Check the maximum length. A few older sites limit passwords to 12 or 15 characters.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 16 character password strong enough?

Yes. A random 16 character password with letters, numbers and symbols has about 100 bits of strength, which is far beyond what current attacks can crack.

Why do some websites reject 16 character passwords?

Some sites limit length or block certain symbols. If a site rejects your password, turn off symbols or use a shorter length.

Should I use 16 characters or more?

16 is a good minimum for important accounts. For a password manager master password, use a longer one.
