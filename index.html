About ALTools

ALTools is a free online tools website that brings simple and useful calculators, converters and generators together in one place. Every tool works directly in your web browser. There is nothing to download or install, and you do not need to create an account.

What You Can Do on ALTools

  • Find your exact age with the Age Calculator.
  • Check your health numbers with the BMI Calculator and the Body Fat Percentage Calculator.
  • Work out percentages and sale prices with the Percentage Calculator and the Discount Calculator.
  • Add or subtract a percentage and find the change between two numbers with the Increase Percentage Calculator.
  • Find the average of several percentages or your overall marks percentage with the Average Percentage Calculator.
  • Create scannable codes with the QR Code Generator.
  • Convert distances with the Unit Converter.
  • Count words and characters with the Word Counter.
  • Create strong random passwords with the Password Generator. You can also try the Random Password Generator, the Strong Password Generator or the 16 Character Password Generator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are ALTools free to use?

Yes. All ALTools calculators, converters and generators are free to use online.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You do not need to register or install anything. Open a tool and use it in your browser.

Are my numbers and passwords saved?

The calculators and the password generator work in your browser and do not save what you enter. The QR Code Generator uses a third-party service to draw the QR image, so do not enter private information there.

Can I use ALTools on my phone?

Yes. Every tool is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Which tools does ALTools have?

ALTools has an age calculator, BMI calculator, body fat percentage calculator, percentage calculator, increase percentage calculator, average percentage calculator, discount calculator, unit converter, word counter, QR code generator and password generator.
