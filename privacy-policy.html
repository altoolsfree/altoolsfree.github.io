Privacy Policy

Learn how ALTools handles information, cookies, advertising and third-party services.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ALTools ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you visit our website and use our free online tools.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information You Enter Into Our Tools

Most ALTools calculators and converters work directly in your web browser. The values and text you enter into tools such as the Age Calculator, BMI Calculator, Percentage Calculator, Word Counter and Unit Converter are not sent to our servers and are not stored by us.

These tools are designed to perform calculations and conversions locally in your browser.

The QR Code Generator uses an external QR service (api.qrserver.com) to create QR code images. The text or link you enter may be sent to that service to generate the QR code. Please do not enter sensitive or confidential information into the QR Code Generator.

The external QR service has its own privacy policy and data practices, which are outside our control.

Log and Technical Information

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record certain technical information when you visit the website. This may include information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the date and time of your visit.

Technical information may be used to operate, maintain and secure the website and to understand how visitors use our services.

Cookies

Cookies are small files that websites may store on your device. ALTools may use cookies or similar technologies to help keep the website working, understand website traffic and support advertising.

Third-party services, including advertising partners, may also use cookies or similar technologies.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how some website features work.

Advertising

ALTools may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks, such as Adsterra.

Advertising partners may collect information such as IP address, browser information and pages viewed. They may use cookies or similar technologies to display advertisements and measure advertising performance.

We do not control the cookies, tracking technologies or data practices used by third-party advertising providers. Please review the privacy policies of the relevant advertising partners for more information.

Third-Party Links and Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or use external services to provide certain features.

We are not responsible for the content, security or privacy practices of websites and services operated by third parties. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before providing them with personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

Your Privacy Choices

  • You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings.
  • You can use browser privacy features to control tracking.
  • You can use an ad-blocking tool if you choose.
  • You can contact ALTools with questions about this Privacy Policy.
