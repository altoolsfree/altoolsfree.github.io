What Is a Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The percentage symbol (%) is commonly used to represent percentages in everyday calculations, finance, shopping, statistics, and mathematics.

How to Calculate a Percentage

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the percentage by the number and divide the result by 100.

For example, 20% of 150 is calculated as (20 × 150) ÷ 100 = 30.

Percentage Formulas

  • Percentage of a number = Percentage × Number ÷ 100
  • What percent is A of B = A ÷ B × 100
  • Percentage change = (New value − Old value) ÷ Old value × 100

For percentage change, you can also use our Percentage Increase Calculator. To find the average of several percentages, try the Average Percentage Calculator.

Percentage Examples

  • 15% of 200 = (15 × 200) ÷ 100 = 30
  • 7.5% of 80 = (7.5 × 80) ÷ 100 = 6
  • 30 out of 150 = 30 ÷ 150 × 100 = 20%

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the percentage by the number and divide the result by 100. For example, 10% of 200 is 20.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example, 30 out of 150 is 30 ÷ 150 × 100 = 20%.

How do I calculate 10% of a number quickly?

Move the decimal point one place to the left. For example, 10% of 250 is 25.

How do I calculate a percentage increase or decrease?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100. For example, a rise from 80 to 100 is (100 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = 25%.
